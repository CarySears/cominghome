How often does this run?
Once a month. Upcoming dates are October 17, November 21, and December 19, and in the new year Anna will continue offering monthly sessions reflective of each season.
Should I book a single workshop or the season?
A single workshop is $47 and is good for any one of the three dates. All three, October through December, are $120, a savings of $21 over booking them one at a time, and a season of self-care: a monthly ritual of resetting and reconnecting with your true self.
